Artificial Intelligence

AI-Powered Features Inside Urban Oasis

Smart engine that understands your garden and responds intelligently

🌱 Recommendation Engine

Matches user inputs (location type, sunlight level, experience, budget) against a plant database and returns the top matching plants with a shopping list and total cost estimate.

🔬 Disease Detection

User uploads a plant photo. The system analyses it and cross-references the disease database. Returns disease name, confidence score %, all symptoms, causes, organic & chemical treatments, and prevention steps.

💧 Smart Water Calculation

AI formula uses 5 live variables — plant type, pot size, current weather, season, and soil type — to calculate the exact water amount needed today with overwatering warnings.

🗓️ Auto Schedule Generation

Based on the plant database, auto-generates a full 30-day care calendar with watering, fertilising, pruning, and harvesting tasks pre-populated for all plant types.

🤖 Live AI Chat Example

My tomato plant leaves are turning yellow 😟
Yellow Leaves — Most Common Causes:

1. Overwatering — check if soil is constantly wet
2. Nitrogen deficiency — apply NPK fertiliser
3. Low light — needs 6+ hours sun
4. Root rot — ensure drainage holes

→ Stick finger 2cm in soil. If wet, stop watering for 3 days.
How do I fix root rot?
Remove plant from pot, trim dark roots, repot in fresh dry soil with drainage holes. Apply cinnamon powder (natural antifungal). Water only when top soil is dry. 🌿
