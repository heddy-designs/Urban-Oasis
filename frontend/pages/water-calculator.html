⚠️ Watering Warnings
💡 Smart Watering Tips
🌅Water in the early morning (6-8 AM) to reduce evaporation and fungal risk.
👆Test soil moisture with your finger — water only when the top 2cm is dry.
🪣Always water at the base of the plant, not on leaves — prevents fungal disease.
🌧️Skip watering after rain — check soil moisture before watering during monsoon.
💨Hot and windy days increase watering frequency by 20-30% due to faster evaporation.

🌅
Early Morning (Recommended)
6:00 AM – 8:00 AM • Best absorption, least evaporation
🌆
Late Evening (Acceptable)

🚫
Midday (Avoid)
