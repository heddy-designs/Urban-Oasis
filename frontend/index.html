Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any gardening experience to use Urban Oasis? ▾
Absolutely not! Urban Oasis is designed specifically for beginners. Our AI walks you through every step — from choosing plants to daily care routines — with simple, easy-to-follow instructions.
What if I only have a small balcony or windowsill? ▾
Perfect! Urban Oasis specializes in small-space gardening. Our Space Planner optimizes even a 4x3 ft balcony with vertical racks and container arrangements to maximize your growing potential.
How accurate is the disease identifier? ▾
Our disease identifier uses AI analysis with an extensive database of plant diseases. It provides diagnosis with confidence levels, symptoms, causes, and both organic and chemical treatment options.
Is Urban Oasis free to use? ▾
Yes! You can sign up and access all 12 core modules completely free. Start growing today with no credit card required.
Can I use Urban Oasis for terrace gardens? ▾
Absolutely! Urban Oasis supports balconies, terraces, indoor spaces, and windowsills. The recommendations and space planner adapt to whichever type of space you have.
